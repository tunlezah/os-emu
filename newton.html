Newton OS 2.1 — MessagePad 2000

Apple Computer · March 1997 · the museum's first mobile exhibit

This is a hand-held computer from 1997: a StrongARM at 162 MHz — the fastest processor Apple shipped in anything that year — behind a 320×480 glass panel showing sixteen shades of grey, run entirely with a pen. There is no boot sequence to watch, because there is no boot: a Newton is instant on, which is the first argument the Mobile wing makes against the rest of this museum.

Your mouse is the stylus. Tap things. Write in the Notepad with the pen and watch the recogniser have opinions. Tap Extras for the drawer of applications, tap the light bulb and write lunch tomorrow, and find the Close box ⊠ where Apple put it: the bottom right corner.
