Palm OS 3.5 — the connected organizer

Palm, Inc. · February 2000 · the machine that won by subtraction

This is a Palm IIIc, the first colour Palm — 160 × 160 pixels, 8 MB of battery-backed RAM, and a processor slower than your keyboard's. It has no filesystem, no Save command anywhere, and no boot sequence: press a hardware button and the application is simply there, exactly as you left it. The handheld computer had already failed twice — PenPoint and Newton, both grander, both better funded — before this design won by refusing to do almost everything.

Your mouse is the stylus (a real one needed both hands — that matters later). Tap the screen. Write in the silkscreened area below it: letters on the left half, numbers on the right, one stroke per character. The Graffiti card button above shows every stroke, drawn from the recogniser's own tables.
