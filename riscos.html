RISC OS 3.11

Acorn Computers · 1992 · 2MB of ROM

The desktop that made different choices. There is no menu bar — not on the screen, not in the windows, nowhere. Every menu in the entire system comes from the middle mouse button, pressed over whatever you are pointing at.

To save a file you are handed a small box with a file icon in it and no path field. You drag the icon into the directory you want it in. That is the whole mechanism.
