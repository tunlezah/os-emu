Which monitor is plugged in?

On a real ST this was not a menu. The machine sensed, at power-on, which monitor was attached, and that decided which screen modes existed for the rest of the session. A colour monitor gave you low and medium resolution and never high; the mono monitor gave you high resolution and nothing else. Choose, and the machine boots.

TOS lives in ROM, so there is nothing to load from disk. Expect the desktop in about two seconds — most of which is the memory test.
