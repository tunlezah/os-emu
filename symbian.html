Nokia N95 · March 2007   vs   the telephone announced in January and sold in June

Nokia N95 — S60 3rd Edition FP1, Symbian OS 9.2

    The other one — June 2007

      And a 3.5-inch capacitive multi-touch screen running a real web browser.
      The N95 won every line but one, and that one line was the entire game. This exhibit exists to demonstrate it physically rather than to assert it: switch the phone on, learn the two softkeys and the navigator — which are genuinely good — and then, in about thirty seconds, open the browser and try to read a page.
      Which N95?  Firmware
      The mouse may press the drawn keys. It may not touch the screen — there is no touch here, anywhere.
